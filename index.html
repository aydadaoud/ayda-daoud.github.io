02 / THE ENGINEER BEHIND THE WORK

HOW I
THINK.

THE THINKING BEHIND THE BUILD.

I have always been more interested in understanding how something works than simply knowing that it does.

I like taking things apart mentally, looking at how the components interact, questioning why something was designed a certain way and wondering whether there is a better solution. I enjoy the technical side of engineering, but I also care about how an idea is developed, presented and brought to life.

Motorsports is where many of these interests come together. There is something fascinating about the relationship between design, performance, precision and teamwork. Every component has a purpose. Every decision has consequences. And there is always something that can be improved.

I don't want to limit myself to designing things on a screen. I want to be involved in the entire process: understanding the problem, developing the concept, working through the calculations, building the prototype, testing it and figuring out what comes next.

I'm still learning, still experimenting and still finding out what I'm capable of building. That's exactly what makes engineering exciting to me.

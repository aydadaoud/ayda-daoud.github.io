02 / The person behind the projects

Engineer by curiosity.

A little more than CAD files

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the American University of Beirut. I like figuring out how things work, but I'm especially interested in what happens after the initial idea: sketching it, designing it, building it, testing it, finding what doesn't work, and trying again.

I enjoy being involved in the whole process. Workshops, prototypes, mechanical systems, vehicle projects, and working with people who are just as excited about making something work as I am.

Engineering is a big part of what I do, but it isn't the only part. Writing, reading, drawing, and music give me other ways to explore ideas. I like having different interests and finding unexpected connections between them.

I'm still figuring out exactly where I want to take my engineering career. Motorsports and automotive engineering are strong interests, but what matters most to me is continuing to learn by doing and working on projects that challenge me.

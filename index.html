WITH PURPOSE.

the hands-on kind ✍

I'm an artist with an engineer's brain. I write, read, draw, sing, and dance, and if it's creative, I'm in. My mind thinks like an engineer, but the way I see things is pure art. I'm always on the creative team and in the room where the main idea gets made.

I adore anything hands-on, because that's where what's going on in my head finally comes out. I'm studying Mechanical Engineering at the American University of Beirut, and I love the point where theory becomes something you can actually build, test, improve, and put to work. I care a lot, and I'm probably the most dedicated person you'll meet. I've been through hell and back, and I'll still get up and keep going. People call me a sunshine. I'm always smiling, even when I'm crying. I'm a ball of energy, and I push everyone around me to be their best.

Family comes first. I don't smoke, I rarely drink or party, and I love my friends and what I do. My life is busy, and I love every part of it.

04 / OUTSIDE THE WORKSHOP

BUILT DIFFERENT.
ALSO VERY BUSY.

Engineering is a big part of my life. It isn't the only thing that keeps me moving.

I'm an engineer by training, an artist by nature, and a little bit of everything in between.

I love creating in every form: writing, reading, drawing, singing, dancing, and turning random ideas into something real. Creativity has always been a huge part of who I am, and I think that's what makes the way I approach engineering a little different. My mind works in mechanisms, structures, and systems, but it also works in colors, stories, possibilities, and ideas that haven't been tried yet.

I'm usually the person throwing out ideas, joining the creative team, or helping turn a completely random thought into an actual project. I love hands-on work because it gives me a way to translate what's happening in my head into something I can see, touch, test, and improve. For me, engineering isn't just about finding the right answer. It's about exploring all the ways you could get there.

I'm also someone who cares deeply about what I do and the people I do it with. I believe in showing up, putting in the work, and giving things everything I've got. I'm persistent, sometimes stubbornly so, and I don't give up just because something gets difficult. I love working with people, pushing ideas further, encouraging those around me, and being part of teams where everyone brings something different to the table.

Outside the workshop, I'm a family-first person, a loyal friend, and someone who somehow manages to fill every spare moment with another activity, idea, or project. I'm often told I bring sunshine and energy wherever I go. I genuinely love being busy, creating, learning, and making the most out of life.

I like to think I'm proof that an analytical mind and an artistic soul can work together. And honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way.

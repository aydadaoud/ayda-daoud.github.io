01 / The person behind the projects

More than
one gear.

An engineer's mind. An artist's soul. A brain with approximately seventeen tabs open.

hi, that's me!

I've never really been good at sticking to just one thing.

I write, read, draw, sing, dance, build, design, experiment, and somehow always find room for one more thing. I love art in all its forms, and creativity has always been my way of understanding and expressing the world around me. It's also what I bring into engineering.

My brain is constantly running: ideas, possibilities, little details, big plans, and sometimes completely random concepts that I just have to try. I'm usually somewhere in the middle of the brainstorming session, throwing ideas around, helping shape the creative direction, or figuring out how we can turn something that exists only in our heads into something real.

That's probably why I love hands-on engineering so much. Give me a workshop, a set of tools, a prototype, and a problem to solve, and I'm happy. I love taking an idea, getting my hands dirty, making mistakes, trying again, and watching it slowly come together.

I'm passionate about the people I make things with. I care deeply, get invested, and give everything I have to the things and people that matter to me. I'm persistent, occasionally stubborn, and I want the people around me to grow, try, and push themselves.

I'm ambitious, curious, creative, occasionally chaotic, and always ready to make something happen. I don't want to choose between being an artist and being an engineer. I want to bring both into everything I do.

People call me sunshine. I'll take it. ☼
